# Code of Conduct

MotionForge is committed to a respectful, technically rigorous community in
which people can participate without harassment.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and evidence rather than people.
- Respect different experience levels, identities, languages, and constraints.
- Accept correction, disclose conflicts of interest, and give credit.
- Protect private reports, credentials, media, and personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, stalking, discrimination, or sexualized attention.
- Personal attacks, insults, sustained disruption, or deliberate intimidation.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Knowingly submitting malicious code, fabricated evidence, or undisclosed
  third-party material.
- Retaliating against someone who raises a good-faith concern.

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, review,
private project channels, events, and public representation of the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to a maintainer using a private contact
method listed on their GitHub profile. If no private method is available, open
a neutral issue asking for a private contact channel without naming people or
including incident details. Security reports should instead follow
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

Include what happened, where and when it happened, relevant context, and the
response you are requesting. Maintainers will limit disclosure, avoid conflicts
of interest, and consider the safety and wishes of affected people.

Depending on severity and history, maintainers may issue a private correction,
formal warning, temporary participation restriction, removal of content or
privileges, or a permanent ban. Immediate protective action does not require a
completed investigation. A person subject to enforcement may make one concise
appeal to a maintainer who was not directly involved, when one is available.

Bad-faith or knowingly false reports may themselves violate this policy;
unproven good-faith reports do not.
