## Behavior and compatibility

The small bold `titleCard` macro previously put 48px padding on a 92px-high plate
at 640×360, leaving zero content height and a 128px title with a 67px fitting
minimum. The compiled Scene, layout and failed real-browser render were preserved
before changing the preset. The renderer correctly rejected that unusable Scene.

Title cards with a known composition now scale their default padding, fonts,
spacing, corner radius and stroke, reserve positive text line boxes, and grow the
plate within its safe area for optional subtitle/kicker content. Line-box allocation
protects a readable minimum before distributing extra height. The existing shared
glyph fitter still measures real text and may reject copy that cannot fit; this
is not a promise that arbitrary copy or caller geometry will fit.

This applies only to the title-card macro and its underlying title-card text
overlay. Composition-less overlays retain their legacy defaults. Caller-provided
geometry, font and fitting overrides still win. Generated groups/text remain
editable with the same IDs and timing. Scene v1, public APIs and extension
contracts are unchanged. Other text-overlay templates and macros are unchanged.
The title-card minimal look also omits its formerly invalid `boxShadow: "none"`.

## Qualification

All 195 existing preset tests passed. A real pinned-Chromium matrix passed 408
bounded cases across six small/HD landscape, portrait and square sizes, all three
looks, title/subtitle/kicker combinations, long Latin/CJK, authored line breaks and
blank optional slots. It checks complete copy, readable fonts, positive contained
boxes and lines, stable IDs/timing, deterministic expansion and editable text.
A separate default-geometry pixel probe checks painted title glyphs after entry.
No golden tolerance was loosened or existing pixel baseline regenerated.

The full aggregate release gate passed locally on macOS 26.6.2 arm64, Node
20.19.5 and pnpm 10.34.5, including exact goldens, rendered glyph probes,
independent FFmpeg media checks, terminal editing, three-browser probes,
resource/performance gates and package smokes. Local retained tarballs, hosted CI
and the final tag-bound publication bundle passed their clean consumer gates.
Workspace and exact release-set production audits reported no vulnerabilities.
Changed files pass formatting; the repository-wide formatter still reports 39
untouched baseline files.
No paid provider or model call is needed for engine qualification. These checks
do not qualify arbitrary fonts, every copy length, agent intent or consumer
deployment. Consumers must adopt the exact final registry bytes independently.
