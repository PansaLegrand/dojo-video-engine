# Kīlauea footage

Credit: **U.S. Geological Survey, Hawaiian Volcano Observatory**. The two
original video records linked below identify their footage as **Public Domain**.
Both record actual eruptions at Kīlauea, Hawaiʻi. The adjacent shots span two
monitoring dates; this edit does not portray a single continuous observation.
USGS did not produce or endorse this DojoClip example.

All three excerpts are silent H.264, 16:9, square pixels, 270 frames at
30 fps. The hero excerpt has no usable recorded sound. No audio from another
eruption has been substituted. No government logo or insignia is included.
