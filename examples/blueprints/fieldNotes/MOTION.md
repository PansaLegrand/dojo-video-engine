# Field Notes

Two or more series reveal on one scale with an exact value table. Preserve ordered art layers and a final readable hold.

Source: [examples/creative-showcase/data-story.json](../../../examples/creative-showcase/data-story.json).

Use the `remixBlueprint` macro with `blueprint: "fieldNotes"`. Inspect the supplied copy on a held frame after changing format. The editable art and text remain separate native nodes.
