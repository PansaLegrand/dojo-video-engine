# Yellowstone footage

Footage and location sound: **National Park Service, Yellowstone National Park**.
The NPS [Yellowstone Video Library](https://www.nps.gov/yell/learn/photosmultimedia/videolibrary.htm)
identifies its footage as public domain and requests credit. The park did not
produce or endorse this DojoClip promo. The following files are excerpts of
real camera recordings, not generated images or simulated camera moves.

The three silent H.264 clips contain 270 frames each at 30 fps. Separating the
ambience lets both cuts use the same sound recording without restarting it
when the hero image changes size. No logo or government insignia was taken
from the park.
